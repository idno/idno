Standard.site Sync

Automatically share your writing with the wider web. When you connect your account, everything you publish here will also appear on apps like Leaflet, Frontpage, and other readers that support standard.site.

Your content stays on your site — syncing just makes it discoverable in more places.

Get started

To start syncing, enter the handle for your AT Protocol account (this is usually your Bluesky handle, like yourname.bsky.social). You'll be taken to your server to approve the connection, then brought back here.

Don't have one? You can create a free account at bsky.app or any AT Protocol provider.
