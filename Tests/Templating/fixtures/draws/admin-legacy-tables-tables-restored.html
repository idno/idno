Legacy tables

When this site was upgraded from Idno 1.x, the tables of the old database were kept, renamed legacy_ followed by their old name. The site no longer reads them. Keep them until you are sure the upgrade went well; then you can delete them here.

This database holds a site already upgraded to this version of Idno and, again, the tables of an Idno 1.x site (entities, entities_metadata, entities_search), for example after the upgrade was undone or a backup restored. Nothing was changed. To upgrade the 1.x site again, remove the tables whose names begin with idno_ first. To keep the upgraded site, remove or rename the 1.x tables.
