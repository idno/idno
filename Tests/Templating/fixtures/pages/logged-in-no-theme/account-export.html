Export your data

You can download an RSS version of everything you've posted on this site. This file is suitable for importing into content management systems like WordPress, or another Idno site.

Platforms like WordPress may assume that all your posts should be displayed publicly. In order to protect your privacy, you may wish to just download your public posts.

Export as WordPress (WXR)

Download a WordPress eXtended RSS (WXR) file. This format includes additional metadata such as post dates, slugs, and comments, and is the standard format for importing into WordPress.

Platforms like WordPress may assume that all your posts should be displayed publicly. In order to protect your privacy, you may wish to just download your public posts.
