Import content

Import your content from other sites into Idno. All imported content will be treated as a post, with a title and body content. Posts that are private or drafts at their source stay private or drafts here, and nothing you import is announced to your followers.

Importing the same file twice creates the posts twice.

WordPress

Upload a WordPress XML file and turn it into Idno posts. An RSS file exported from another Idno site can be imported here too. How do I get my WordPress XML file?

To get your WordPress XML file:

  1. Log into your WordPress site
  2. Click on Tools
  3. Click on Export
  4. Click to export posts
  5. Click Download Export File


Blogger

Upload a Blogger XML file and turn it into Idno posts. How do I get my Blogger XML file?

To get your Blogger XML file:

  1. Log into Blogger, and click on your blog title
  2. Click on Settings
  3. Click on Other
  4. Click on Export Posts at the top of your page
  5. Click on Export Blog
